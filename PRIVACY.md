# Privacy Policy — Health of a Titan (HoT)

**Last updated: 2026-11-05**

Health of a Titan (HoT) is a private, single-user application used by its owner to
access their own WHOOP data through the WHOOP API for personal health analysis.

## Data we access
Recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts, and basic profile — only for the account owner
who authorizes access via WHOOP OAuth.

## How it is used
Solely for the owner's personal health analysis and insights.

## Sharing
Data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

## Storage
Data and access tokens are kept privately on the owner's own devices and are not published.

## Retention and revocation
The owner can revoke access at any time in WHOOP account settings, and stored data
can be deleted at any time.
